Struggling with Dynamic Programming? 🤔 Don't worry, we've got you covered! DP is a fundamental technique in algorithms that can transform exponential time complexities into polynomial ones.

This post breaks down the core concepts:
🌟 **Overlapping Subproblems:** When the same subproblems are computed multiple times.
🚀 **Optimal Substructure:** When an optimal solution can be built from optimal subproblem solutions.

We also compare **Memoization (Top-Down)** and **Tabulation (Bottom-Up)** approaches with a classic example: the Fibonacci sequence!
