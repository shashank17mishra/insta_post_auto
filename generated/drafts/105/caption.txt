Unlocking the secrets of Graph Traversal! 🚀 Ever wondered how Google Maps finds the shortest route or how social networks connect people? It's all thanks to algorithms like Breadth-First Search (BFS) and Depth-First Search (DFS)! 

BFS explores level by level, perfect for finding the shortest path in unweighted graphs. DFS dives deep into one path before backtracking, ideal for maze solving and topological sorting.
