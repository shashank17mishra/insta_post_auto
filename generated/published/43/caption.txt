Dive into the fascinating world of Machine Learning! 🤖 Ever wondered how AI learns? It all starts with understanding Supervised vs. Unsupervised Learning. These are the foundational pillars of almost every ML application you see today!

💡 Supervised Learning: Think of it as learning with a teacher, where you have correct answers (labels) to guide you. Perfect for predictions like spam detection or house price forecasting.

🔍 Unsupervised Learning: This is like exploring without a map, discovering hidden patterns and structures in data all on your own. Great for customer segmentation or anomaly detection.
